02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTSSTILL LEARNING. ALWAYS ASKING.
A WORK IN PROGRESS, IN THE BEST WAY.

An engineer’s rigor.
A writer’s curiosity.
A product ambition.

I’m from Bhadrak, Odisha. I studied mining engineering at NIT Rourkela, then followed questions that took me into research, analytics and AI. I like finding the thread between things that initially seem unrelated.

Today, I’m developing the back end of AI agents, chatbots and voice systems. Longer term, I want to grow into the AI product domain: building with technical depth while understanding the business, the user and the reason a product should exist.

I’m particularly curious about urban informatics. Cities bring together people, infrastructure, behaviour and data. I want to learn how AI and thoughtful products can help make those systems easier to understand and navigate.

And I love to write. It gives me a way to slow down, question an assumption and turn a half-formed idea into something another person can understand.

NOTES / 01

Writing is how I connect the dots.

I want to explore the overlap between AI, business, cities and everyday decisions. I’m interested in the kind of writing that makes a reader see a familiar problem from a different angle.

When does an AI feature become a useful product? +

I want to write about the distance between a model that works and a product that helps: the user’s context, the workflow around it, and the judgement needed to decide what good looks like.

What can cities teach us about systems? +

Movement, infrastructure and human behaviour are connected. Urban informatics interests me as a way to ask better questions about how those connections shape daily life.

How can complex work become a clear story? +

A chart or a model is only part of the explanation. I want to get better at showing the reasoning, uncertainty and human meaning behind a technical result.
