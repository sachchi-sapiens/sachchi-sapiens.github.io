01 · AI · Nov 2026

Medical Docs Bot

Medical records are scattered: paper prescriptions, lab reports in different apps, histories nobody can find. This app reads prescriptions and lab reports from photos or PDFs with a vision LLM, checks the output with Pydantic and files each one by type, body system and date.

A LangGraph agent then finds the right documents, looks up each medicine's FDA label and pulls past test values before it answers, citing the files it used. It never diagnoses and never tells anyone to change a medicine.

  • $0.0006per document read
  • 5FastAPI endpoints
  • Localbge-small embeddings

Cities that answer back

Cities hand us the same problems every day: bad air, heat, and the power load of air conditioners trying to keep up. I want to put LLMs on top of air-quality, heat and power data so a resident or a planner can ask a plain question and get a straight answer with sources. Urban informatics, made usable.
